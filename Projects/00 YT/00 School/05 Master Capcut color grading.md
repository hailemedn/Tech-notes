# 05 Master Capcut color grading

---
Under adjust tab

## COLOR
## Temp
- cooler or warmer, 
- blue or red
## Tint
- Green or magenta
- Green or red and Blue
## Saturation
- Intensity of all the colors
- black and white to very colorful

## LIGHT
## Exposure
- Overall brightness
## Contrast
	- difference bn dark and bright areas


- Examine the clips and apply the opposite
- Too warm, slide down the temp
- too bright, slide down the exposure etc..

- Always better to under expose than to over expose when shooting, much easier to fix.


## Hue, Saturation, brightness
- Manipulate a selected single color

## Luts
Try the creative ones

## Effects
- luminance, under the effect tab, give the footage a dreamy look
